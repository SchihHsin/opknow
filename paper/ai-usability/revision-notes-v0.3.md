关于“记录不足”的意见，本次用可检查的材料及覆盖表纠正笼统表述。工具交付文本、完整开发者最终答案和硬件执行结果仍分别说明，避免用一种材料替代另一种。O的有效材料先于导航页检查；E的首次错误来自工具服务，均已按事件记录纠正。

The revision replaces blanket archive-deficiency statements with an explicit account of retained material and coverage. Tool-delivered text, final developer-facing answers and execution outcomes remain distinct evidence objects. O's useful return predates its navigation-only check; E's first error is a tool-service overload.

尚未新增独立分析者应用、工程执行或界面效果评价；这些问题不能以编辑修订或文件检查冒充完成。当前稿件的证据是更具体的方法应用与分析性论证，仍需由评审判断其贡献是否足够。

Independent analyst application, engineering execution and interface-outcome evaluation have not been added. File integrity checks do not substitute for these studies. The revised contribution is supported by a more explicit application and analytic argument.
